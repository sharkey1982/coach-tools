▸ Group

"Group" decides which of the four buckets an activity slots into everywhere else in the app — Session Builder's slots (defaults to Movement skills + Dribbling, with Ball Striking and Match Play addable), the Activities page filter pills, and the Syllabus/Video Admin dropdowns. Within Movement skills, tick "Tag variation" for anything that's fundamentally a tag game (freeze/chase mechanic), even if it layers on a skill like dribbling — leave it unticked for other movement games (reaction, awareness, agility). Match Play isn't auto-assigned; tag it by hand for small-sided/game-format activities like Formation/Pitch Rotation or Create Your Own Corner. "Focus label" is just the descriptive chip text shown on the card — keep it specific (e.g. "Dribbling · Themed") rather than the generic group name. Saving here commits straight to the activities file and redeploys — changes take about a minute to show up everywhere; this page and the table below reflect the change immediately.
