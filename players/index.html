▸ Pairings & teams

Each pitch hosts one match: Team 1 v Team 2. Pick the week, match and pitch this fixture is for, tick who's available, then assign each player to Team 1 or Team 2 and choose their position — the pitch diagram updates to match, and team size flexes with however many you assign (2v2 up to 5v5). Ability group and a 🔁 hint (played together before) are shown to help you decide. Save records this pitch's two teams; switch pitch above and repeat for other matches happening at the same time.

▸ Teams history

Every saved week's match-ups, most recent first — who was on which team, and which pitch it was played on.

▸ Pairing history

Who a player has already been on a team with, based on the weeks you've saved above — so you can spread pairings out over time. If things get out of sync, saved sessions can be removed below.

▸ Attendance

Tick who turned up for a syllabus week and save it — re-saving the same week overwrites that week's record rather than duplicating it. Uses the Discipline/Group filters from Existing players above to pick the roster.
