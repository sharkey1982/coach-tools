▸ Pairings & teams

Tick who's present today, decide how many teams (or how big each team should be), then assign each player yourself — ability group and a 🔁 hint (played together before) are shown to help you decide. Uses the Discipline/Group filters above, plus the ability pool below, to pick the player pool. "Formation view" lays teams out on a pitch (3-a-side 1-1-1, or 5-a-side 1-2-1 with a keeper) so you can place each player in a specific role, not just a team.

▸ Pairing history

Who a player has already been on a team with, based on the weeks you've saved above — so you can spread pairings out over time. If things get out of sync, saved sessions can be removed below.
