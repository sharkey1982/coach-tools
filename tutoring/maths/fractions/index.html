Question 1

What fraction of the bar below is shaded?

3 of the 5 equal boxes are shaded: 3/5.

Question 2

1/2 = ?/4

Multiply top and bottom by the same number.

4 ÷ 2 = 2, so multiply top and bottom by 2: 1/2 = 2/4.

Question 3

Which is bigger: 1/3 or 1/4?

Splitting the same-size bar into fewer, bigger pieces makes each piece larger: 1/3 is bigger than 1/4.

Simplifying a fraction

8/12 ÷4 = 2/3
Divide the numerator and denominator by their highest common factor (here, 4) until they share no common factor.

Practice

Question 4

3/8 + 2/8 =

Same denominator — add the numerators: 3 + 2 = 5. Answer: 5/8.

Question 5

7/9 − 4/9 =

Check if the answer simplifies.

7 − 4 = 3, giving 3/9 — which simplifies (÷3) to 1/3.

Question 6

Simplify 8/12 to its lowest terms.

The highest common factor of 8 and 12 is 4: 8÷4=2, 12÷4=3. Answer: 2/3.

Question 7

2 1/5 + 1 2/5 =

Add the whole numbers and the fractions separately.

Whole numbers: 2 + 1 = 3. Fractions: 1/5 + 2/5 = 3/5. Answer: 3 3/5.

Multiplying and dividing fractions

multiply: 3/4 × 2/3 = (3×2)/(4×3) = 6/12 = 1/2
divide by a whole number: 4/5 ÷ 2 = 4/(5×2) = 4/10 = 2/5
Multiply straight across (numerator × numerator, denominator × denominator). To divide by a whole number, multiply the denominator by it instead.

Question 11

3/4 × 2/3 =

Question 12

4/5 ÷ 2 =

4/(5×2) = 4/10 = 2/5.

Question 13

3 1/2 − 1 3/4 =

Convert both to improper fractions first — borrowing doesn't work cleanly here.

3 1/2 = 7/2 = 14/4. 1 3/4 = 7/4. 14/4 − 7/4 = 7/4 = 1 3/4.

Tip: before subtracting mixed numbers, check whether the fraction part of the first number is smaller than the second — if so, convert both to improper fractions rather than trying to "borrow" from the whole number, which is where most mistakes happen.
