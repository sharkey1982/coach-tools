Fractions

Direct calculation · Y3–Y6

This page is straight fraction arithmetic — the kind that appears as "Work out: 3/4 − 1/6" on an 11+ paper, not word problems. For fraction-of-an-amount word problems (e.g. "Sarah spends 3/8 of her £48"), use Bar Models instead — that's a different skill. Multiplying and dividing fractions

multiply: 3/4 × 2/3 = (3×2)/(4×3) = 6/12 = 1/2
divide by a whole number: 4/5 ÷ 2 = 4/(5×2) = 4/10 = 2/5
Multiply straight across. To divide by a whole number, multiply the denominator by it instead.
