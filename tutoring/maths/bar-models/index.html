Bar Models

Visualising word problems · Y3–Y6

Bar models are for part-whole word problems: sharing, ratio, comparison, and fraction/percentage of an amount. Draw the bar before writing any sum — split it into equal boxes, work out what one box is worth, then multiply up. This page is the technique only; for fast mental arithmetic use Number Bonds, and for direct fraction calculations (not word problems) use Fractions.

Question 1

A pizza is cut into 4 equal slices. Tom eats 3 of them. What fraction of the pizza is left?

Draw a bar with 4 equal boxes and shade the slices Tom ate.

3 of the 4 boxes are eaten, so 1 box is left: 1/4 of the pizza remains.

There are 8 red counters and 5 blue counters in a bag. How many counters altogether?

Question 5

In a school, there are 34 more girls than boys. There are 210 pupils in total. How many boys are there?

Draw two bars lined up on the left; mark the extra 34 at the end of the shorter one.

Take away the extra 34 first: 210 − 34 = 176, which is 2 equal shares. So boys = 176 ÷ 2 = 88.

Question 6

Amir and Priya share £72 in the ratio 3 : 5. How much does each of them get?

Draw a bar with 3 boxes for Amir and 5 boxes for Priya — 8 boxes in total.

3 + 5 = 8 boxes represent £72, so 1 box = £72 ÷ 8 = £9. Amir = 3 × £9 = £27. Priya = 5 × £9 = £45.

Comparison and age problems 11+ style

Question 9

Ben has £18 more than Alex. Together they have £96. How much does each person have?

Draw two bars lined up on the left; mark the £18 gap at the end of the shorter one.

Take away the extra £18 first: £96 − £18 = £78, which is 2 equal shares. So each share = £39. Alex = £39. Ben = £39 + £18 = £57.

Question 10

Mia is 4 years older than her brother Leo. In 6 years' time, the sum of their ages will be 30. How old is Mia now?

The 4-year gap between Mia and Leo never changes, even in the future.

In 6 years their ages still differ by 4. Sum then = 30, so take away the gap first: 30 − 4 = 26, split into 2 shares of 13. Leo (in 6 yrs) = 13, Mia (in 6 yrs) = 17. Subtract 6 years: Leo now = 7, Mia now = 11.

13 (Leo, in 6 yrs)
13
+4
Mia's bar (bottom) = Leo's bar + the fixed 4-year gap. Mia is now 11, Leo is now 7.

Tip: on every "more than" or "age" question, look for what stays fixed (a difference or a gap) versus what changes (a total) — that's usually the key to which bar to draw first.
