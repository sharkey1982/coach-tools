Bar Models

Visualising word problems · Y3–Y6

Bar models are for part-whole word problems: sharing, ratio, comparison, and fraction/percentage of an amount. Draw the bar before writing any sum — split it into equal boxes, work out what one box is worth, then multiply up. This page is the technique only; for fast mental arithmetic use Number Bonds, and for direct fraction calculations use Fractions. Every question has a New numbers button.
