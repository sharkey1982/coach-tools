Number Bonds

Fast mental maths · Y3–Y6
🎮 Play the game

Number bonds are a pure fluency drill — instant recall of pairs that make a round total. They don't solve word problems on their own, but they make every other technique faster: the "work out what one box is worth, then multiply up" step in a bar model is only quick if the addition and subtraction behind it is instant. Aim for well under 3 seconds per answer.
