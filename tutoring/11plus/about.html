Absolute minimum standardised score for any CSSE offer: 303. Below this, no offer is made regardless of school (appeals and special considerations sit outside this rule).

How the Standardised Score Works

Raw English and Maths scores are each standardised to a mean of 100 and SD of 15, then combined 1:1 with an optional age adjustment for younger candidates:

standardised = ((raw − μ) ÷ σ) × 15 + 100
total = 1.5 × [(std. Maths + days-younger × Maths adj.) + (std. English + days-younger × English adj.)]

A candidate scoring exactly average on both papers, born on the base date, always totals 300.

Enter a total standardised score to see how it compares with recent historical cut-offs. This is guidance only — actual cut-offs are only known after National Offer Day and shift year to year.

Bands are derived from the last four years' cut-offs (2019–2023 entry, the years with clean published breakdowns) using the CSSE's own four-tier methodology: below minimum pass / offered to some in the last 4 years / offered to everyone at this level in the last 4 years / comfortably above what's needed.
